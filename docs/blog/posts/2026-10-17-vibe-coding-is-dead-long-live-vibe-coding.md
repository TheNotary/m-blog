open a new session, disclose my observations to a cheap model in Ask mode, and the exact nature of the problem is disclosed to me.  This happens a lot more now, you've probably noticed too. Closed source is dead btw.  It always has been for me, and a lot of us, but this is the nail in the coffin.  No one's going to want to put up with your vibe coded slop without being able to token-efficiently fix the problems your slop factory has produced. And by that time, thousands of my users will have fixed a bug preventing their CLI from authenticating correctly with the backend because a council of experts decided we should start supporting GMail as an auth provider.
